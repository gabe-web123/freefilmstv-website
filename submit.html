3. Revenue Sharing

FFTV and Filmmaker agree to split ad revenue generated by the Film across all applicable platforms. The specific revenue split percentage will be agreed upon in writing before the Film is published. If no revenue split is agreed upon in writing prior to publication, the default split shall be 50% to FFTV and 50% to Filmmaker.

FFTV does not guarantee that the Film will generate any ad revenue. Filmmaker acknowledges that it is possible the Film may generate little to no revenue.

Revenue reports shall be provided monthly, subject to revenue being generated by the respective content. If the Film has not generated any ad revenue in a given month, FFTV is under no obligation to provide a report for that period. Payments will be issued on a quarterly basis within 30 days of the end of each calendar quarter, only if the Film has generated ad revenue during that quarter. If no revenue has been generated, no payment is due and FFTV is not in breach of this Agreement for not issuing one.

If FFTV fails to issue a payment for revenue that has been generated within 30 days of the quarterly due date, Filmmaker may provide written notice of the overdue payment. FFTV shall have 15 days from receipt of such notice to cure the delinquency. If FFTV fails to cure within the 15-day period, Filmmaker may request removal of the Film per Section 5, and the minimum term requirement shall be waived for that request.
